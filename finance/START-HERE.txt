FINANCE TRACKER - START HERE (Windows)

Everything stays on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere except the Google Sheet you choose to link.

ONE TIME SETUP
1. Install Python: go to python.org/downloads, download it, run it, and TICK "Add python to PATH".
   Then restart the computer once.
2. Before unzipping: right-click the downloaded zip > Properties > tick "Unblock" > OK.
   (This stops Windows from blocking the files.) Then right-click the zip > Extract All, to a
   simple folder such as C:\FinanceApp  (not inside OneDrive or Google Drive).
3. Open that folder and double-click  setup.bat .
   A black window opens and prints steps [1/4] to [4/4]. The install step takes 2 to 5 minutes and
   shows moving text; that is normal. Wait for the word DONE.
   - If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. Double-click  first-run-rules.bat  (only if it is in this folder). It sets up your income rules.
5. Your old budget sheet: in Google Sheets choose File > Download > Microsoft Excel (.xlsx).
   Then DRAG that downloaded file onto the icon  import-old-sheet.bat . It shows a preview.
   Type Y and press Enter to save.
6. Double-click  dashboard.bat . Your browser opens the dashboard. Keep the black window open while you use it.
   - Balances tab: type today's balances (the dollar rate is fetched for you).
   - Google Sheet tab: follow the 5 steps once to link your shared sheet.

EVERY TWO MONTHS (or monthly)
1. Download your statements and drop the files into  C:\Users\<you>\Finance\inbox
2. Double-click  import.bat
3. Double-click  dashboard.bat  then:
   Review tab -> approve categories.  Needs attention tab -> comment on anything odd.
   Imports tab -> Submit.  (The Google Sheet updates by itself.)

IF A WINDOW OPENS AND NOTHING SEEMS TO HAPPEN
- Wait a few minutes during step [3/4]: it is downloading and installing.
- If a window flashes and closes, or you see nothing: open the app folder in File Explorer, click the
  address bar at the top, type  cmd  and press Enter. In the black window type  setup.bat  and press
  Enter. Now any error stays on screen.
- Take a photo or copy the text and send it to me.
- Your data is backed up automatically in the "backups" folder before every import.
